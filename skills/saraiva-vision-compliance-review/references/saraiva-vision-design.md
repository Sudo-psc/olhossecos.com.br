# Saraiva Vision design reference (summary)

Source: public CSS from https://saraivavision.com.br. Use as alignment targets,
not as a strict copy.

## Typography

- Primary: Inter (with Sora/Outfit as optional alternatives in some contexts).
- Use clean, modern sans serif styling with strong hierarchy and bold headings.

## Core colors (hex)

- Primary teal: #0891b2
- Dark teal: #0e7490
- Deep teal: #155e75
- Medical blue: #3b82f6
- Supporting accents: #9333ea, #ec4899
- Cyan accents: #06b6d4, #0ea5e9
- Neutrals: #f8fafc, #f1f5f9, #e2e8f0, #cbd5e1
- Dark text: #0f172a, #1f2937, #334155
- Soft highlight: #ecfeff

## Surfaces and effects

- Glassmorphism: translucent white panels with blur (10-24px), soft borders,
  and layered shadows.
- Gradients: soft multi-stop gradients on hero and cards, with blue/purple/pink
  accents.
- Cards: rounded corners (12-24px), soft shadow stacks, hover lift/scale.

## CTAs and buttons

- Primary CTA uses teal gradient (#0891b2 to #0e7490), white text, 10-12px radius.
- Button sizes: min height ~48px, medium weight text (15-18px), clear focus ring.

## Motion

- Entry animations: slide-up, scale-in.
- Subtle glow or shimmer for emphasis.
- Hover: lift and shadow, underline gradients on links.

## Layout and spacing

- Generous vertical padding (4-8rem sections).
- Wide content width, rounded sections, soft backgrounds.

## Brand alignment checks

- Prefer teal/blue accents over unrelated palettes.
- Use glass panels and gradient highlights to match the site atmosphere.
- Keep tone calm, clinical, and premium (no loud marketing visuals).
